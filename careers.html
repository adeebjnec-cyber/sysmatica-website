— How we hire

Our hiring process.

A transparent and efficient recruitment process, designed to find the right people for our engineering work.

  1. 01

    Application Review

    Our team reviews every application and CV, and shortlists candidates based on qualifications and experience.

  2. 02

    Phone Screening

    Shortlisted candidates receive a call to discuss their background, expectations and initial fit for the role.

  3. 03

    Technical Interview

    An in-depth technical discussion with our engineering leads to understand your skills, knowledge and problem-solving.

  4. 04

    Final Interview

    A conversation with management about the role, your career goals, compensation and next steps.

  5. 05

    Offer & Onboarding

    Selected candidates receive an offer letter, followed by an onboarding programme to get started with the team.
