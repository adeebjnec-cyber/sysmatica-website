— About SysMatica

SysMatica Mechatronics is a leading Indian manufacturer of industrial automation systems, special purpose machines, precision components, tooling, gauges, jigs & fixtures, industrial fabrication, conveyors and mechanical assemblies.

Since 2018.

Engineering excellence with advanced manufacturing.

Founded with the vision of delivering innovative engineering solutions, SysMatica has established itself as a trusted partner for industries seeking high-quality automation, precision manufacturing and customised engineering products.

Our expertise combines mechanical engineering, industrial automation, CNC manufacturing, tool design and precision fabrication to provide complete turnkey manufacturing solutions under one roof — from concept development and machine design to manufacturing, assembly, testing and installation.

Today, SysMatica serves customers from automotive, aerospace, electrical, heavy engineering, consumer appliances, medical devices, renewable energy and process industries.

Special Purpose Machines

Built for one operation. Built to do it perfectly.

Special purpose machines are built for one specific operation or product rather than general use. They deliver high speed, repeatability and a low cost per part for dedicated production — designed around your component and your cycle time.

Applications

  1. Drilling, tapping, boring and milling of automotive and engineering parts
  2. Assembly, testing and leak-test stations
  3. Press-fit, riveting, marking and cutting operations
  4. Dedicated production lines for high-volume parts

Types

Multi-spindle drilling and tapping machinesBoring and facing machinesAssembly and press-fit machinesTesting and inspection machinesHydraulic, pneumatic and servo-driven SPMsTransfer lines and indexing machines

Jigs & Fixtures

Every part held the same way, every time.

Jigs and fixtures hold and position parts accurately so each one is machined, welded or inspected the same way — manual toggle-clamp, pneumatic and modular fixtures on precision base plates. They improve repeatability, shorten cycle times and reduce rework.

Applications

  1. CNC machining and drilling fixtures
  2. Welding fixtures for automotive and fabricated assemblies
  3. Inspection and checking fixtures
  4. Assembly-line positioning and holding
